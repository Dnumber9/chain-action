Two honest limits. First, this node trims logs below block 32,790,343, so no pre-bond fill is directly observable; curve cost is modelled from the bonding curve, and each wallet's position on that curve is inferred from the order it first appears at the pool. A wallet that bought early on the curve but only touched the pool later will show a cost that is too high, making its multiple a floor. Second, identified EOA curve holders account for roughly $10.9k of the $20k raise; the remainder passed through router and aggregator contracts and cannot be attributed to an end wallet.
Post-bond figures are exact: 80,966 pool buys and 43,016 pool sells priced against 127,122 Sync reserve snapshots, blocks 32,790,343 to 34,649,500. Unrealised marked at $2,417 per 1M. Cielo does not index Stable, so its profiles show these wallets on other chains, which is the cross-chain identity check worth running before you follow any of them.
